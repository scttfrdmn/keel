  one 16-lane A-window load is in bounds only when MR*U >= 16 and covers every index the

  per row per k-step, putting exactly 16 of them live at once against the 15 SIMD values
  go1.26.x offers (T10). Emittable and in-budget are mutually exclusive under the shipped
  A-panel layout, so this form has no zero-spill shape at all.
