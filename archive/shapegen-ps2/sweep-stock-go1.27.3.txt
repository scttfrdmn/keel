  none can be, and the constraints that exclude them are arithmetic rather than empirical:
  one 16-lane A-window load is in bounds only when MR*U >= 16 and covers every index the
  body needs only when MR*U <= 16, so MR*U == 16 — and the form hoists one index vector
  per row per k-step, putting exactly 16 of them live at once against the 15 SIMD values
  go1.26.x offers (T10). Emittable and in-budget are mutually exclusive under the shipped
  A-panel layout, so this form has no zero-spill shape at all.

  attributed to `2x64 u=2` in this form: that shape guarantees only 4 A floats, so whatever
  kernel produced 4.438 did not read its A panel the way the shipped kernels read theirs.
  Ruled 2026-08-18 (#107): the threshold is now 4.625, the best figure an emittable
  zero-spill shape reaches, and both gates reconcile it against -frontier rather than
